Selected academic books

  • The Ontic Snap

    How Experience becomes a World

    An illustrated inquiry into how experience shapes perception, recognition, and confidence. Everyday examples explore how interpretations become the world we act within—and how we can revise mistaken judgments while retaining dependable knowledge.

    Kindle eBook Paperback Hardcover

  • The Distortive Invisibility of Counterfactuals

    Missing Comparisons, Misplaced Confidence, and Human Decision-Making

    Why do successful outcomes so often inspire more confidence than they warrant? Thought experiments, historical cases, and a practical worksheet explore how overlooked alternatives distort decisions—and how to identify the comparisons that matter.

    Kindle eBook Paperback Hardcover

  • The Objective Morality Project

    An Expedition to Build a Moral System Everyone Has Reason to Obey

    A fictional commission tests whether moral reasons can bind people independently of their concerns. Sustained dialogue examines welfare, agency, basic reasons, and moral anti-realism, supported by diagrams, argument reconstructions, and further reading.

    Kindle eBook Paperback Hardcover

  • How to Test a “Moral” System

    A Field Guide to Metaethics, Value Disagreement, and the Architecture of Values

    Thirteen tests examine the assumptions, scope, consistency, and practical consequences of moral systems. Comparisons across ethical traditions and cases involving institutions, artificial intelligence, and everyday conflict offer a framework for evaluating moral claims and revising them.

    Kindle eBook Paperback Hardcover
