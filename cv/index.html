Advisory & coaching

Practical support for learning, communication, and inquiry

Individual coaching, focused advice, and educational program design for learners, researchers, educators, and professionals. Work can focus on a single challenge or combine several areas.

Language & communication

Private English coaching, business and medical English, presentations, negotiation, and preparation for overseas graduate and MBA study. Develop language you can use confidently in academic and professional settings.

Academic & technical writing

Editing and rewriting research papers, strengthening argument structure, and preparing conference presentations. Make complex ideas clear while preserving their meaning and the evidence behind them.

Critical thinking & inquiry

Evaluate evidence, analyze arguments, identify logical fallacies and cognitive biases, and compare competing hypotheses. Practice assigning confidence in proportion to the evidence and revising it as you learn.

Education & curriculum design

Design learning programs, teaching materials, English-medium courses, and customized institutional training. Connect learning objectives with practical activities, useful feedback, and independent practice.

Practical AI coaching

Develop prompts, choose useful applications, and coordinate AI tools for learning, writing, and research. Build workflows that include source checking, critical evaluation, and human review.

Professional development

Strengthen cross-cultural communication, negotiation, and presentation skills. Prepare for international leadership roles and the communication demands of working across professional and cultural settings.
